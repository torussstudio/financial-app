## Current boundaries

- Live (non-demo) invoices are checked on issue instead of blocked: company name and address and client name are required; GST and zero-rated invoices also need a valid company GSTIN whose first two digits match the company state code, the client address, a valid client GSTIN when one is given, an HSN/SAC on every line of a B2B invoice, and a number of at most 16 characters (letters, digits, `-`, `/`); exports under LUT also need the company LUT number, valid on the invoice date. Credit notes follow the same rule as their invoice, and sales debit notes are checked like invoices. E-invoicing is preparation only: with "E-invoicing applicable" on, B2B/export invoices export IRP JSON (schema 1.1) for upload on the IRP or a GSP, and the IRN, acknowledgement and signed QR pasted back are printed and cannot be changed; live B2B issue warns (does not block) until the IRN is recorded. No IRP/GSP/GSTN call is made. E-way bills are not implemented. Unallocated live client advances remain gated.
- Preview invoices are watermarked and use fictional data. GST returns are working drafts for accountant review, not filed returns. Field mappings that could not be confirmed against the GSTN offline tool / IRP schema are listed in the GST returns tab (and in `apps/api/src/gst-returns.ts`); verify them before uploading anything.
- Schema version 4 adds: company TAN, LUT number/validity, B2CL threshold (default 2,50,000 — the limit for invoices from 1 Aug 2024 is 1,00,000; confirm and set it), e-invoicing flag; document reverse charge, self-invoice number, zero-rated type, exchange rate and INR totals, IRN fields (immutable by trigger); allocation foreign amount; journal entry `tds` details; append-only `gst_filings`. New accounts: 1250 TDS receivable, 2130/2140/2150 RCM CGST/SGST/IGST payable, 2400 TDS payable, 4200 Foreign exchange gain, 5700 Foreign exchange loss. Startup stops if any of these codes already exists as a different account type.
- The `BILL` document type is the non-GST **Payment Receipt**: issuing it posts the amount received to Petty cash (Cash) or Operating bank (Bank Transfer/Cheque) against the chosen income account. Supplier bills are entered as the separate `PURCHASE` (Vendor bill) type under Purchases & bills; posting one debits the chosen expense/asset account and eligible input GST and credits Accounts payable. Payment receipts issued by earlier builds were posted as Rent expense / Accounts payable and need an accountant-reviewed correcting journal.
- Invoices and payment receipts get their series number when issued (drafts show a `DRAFT-` reference), so abandoned drafts leave no gaps. Automatic numbering skips numbers already used manually.
- Foreign-currency invoices are issued with an exchange rate entered on issue and post their INR equivalent; an advance on a foreign-currency invoice must be recorded as a receipt after issue. Foreign-currency vendor bills and payment receipts are not supported. An advance entered on an INR invoice is posted as a receipt allocated to that invoice when it is issued.
- Unallocated client advances are demo-only. Existing advances cannot yet be allocated to invoices, and vendor advances/refunds are not supported.
- Corrections never delete or edit posted records. Vouchers, receipts, payments and TDS deposits are reversed (a reversed receipt adds negative allocations dated on the reversal, so the invoice becomes outstanding again; TDS and exchange-difference lines reverse with it). Issued invoices are corrected with credit notes (under-charges with sales debit notes): line quantities at the invoice's own rates and tax, limited to the outstanding balance; if money was refunded, reverse the receipt first. Drafts, quotations/proformas and payment receipts can be cancelled; cancelling an issued payment receipt, vendor bill, purchase debit note or unsettled sales debit note posts a reversal. Issued invoices and credit notes cannot be cancelled, so a wrong IRN is corrected on the IRP and with a credit note and new invoice.
- The day book and CSV export show at most the latest 250 selected entries. Bank lines are limited to 250 and audit events to 100. Totals are calculated from the underlying matching ledger, not only visible rows. Full exports/pagination are needed before operational scale.
- Cash/bank, trial balance and balance sheet are company-wide as of the end date; activity charts/P&L use the selected date range and client/category filters. Project profit includes direct postings, without allocation of shared costs.
- Recurring confirmation is manual and produces an actual posted entry; no unattended scheduler runs. Budgets and recurring confirmations use the month containing the selected end date (the current month by default); budget actuals are all postings in that month. Reversing a recurring entry frees that month for confirmation again and removes any bank match.
- Authentication and financial rules have tests, but this is not an independent security audit. Mobile breakpoints are implemented; device-specific QA and accessibility testing remain.
